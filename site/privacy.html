Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20

Data the bot stores

  • Your Discord user ID.
  • The timezone you choose.
  • Your event subscriptions. For each event, the bot stores up to 2 lead times in minutes.
  • Server settings that an admin sets: the reminder channel, the server timezone and the admin role.

Data the bot does not use

The bot does not read message content. It uses no privileged Discord intents.

Direct messages

The bot sends your reminders as direct messages (DMs) to you.

Use and sharing

The bot uses this data only to send reminders. The operator does not sell or share the data.

Deletion

To remove your data, unsubscribe from your events or ask the operator. Data for a server can be removed when the bot leaves it.

See also the Terms of Service.
